# Introducing Callouts: A New Safety Feature

We are excited to announce a major new feature for Subterra: **Callouts**.

Safety is paramount in caving, and keeping track of who is underground and when they are expected back is critical. With the new Callout feature, you can now:

1. **Log Your Trip Details**: Record exactly where you are going, who is with you, and where you are parking.
2. **Set a Callout Time**: Specify when you expect to be out.
3. **Emergency Info**: Provide critical details like car registration and phone numbers to aid rescuers if needed.

If you don't check in by your designated time, the emergency services are contacted by our duty officer, who can then access the information you provided to initiate a rescue.

Head over to the More->Safety Callout section to start using this feature on your next trip!

Stay safe underground! 🔦
