# Book Your Cave Permits on Subterra

You can now **book cave permits directly on Subterra**. No more emails back and forth or hunting for a form: pick a date, pick your group, and request your slot in a few taps.

### Live now: Redhouse Lane Swallet 🕳️
The first cave to go live with online booking is **[Redhouse Lane Swallet](/caves/forest_redhouse-lane-swallet)** in the Forest of Dean.

After years of digging, the diggers have explored Redhouse Lane to an incredible **12km of passage**, and it is now open for tourist trips. Permits are limited, so booking ahead is essential.

### How it works
1. Open the [cave page](/caves/forest_redhouse-lane-swallet) and press **Book Now** in the permit box.
2. Choose an available date on the booking calendar. Slots that are already taken or pending are shown so you can see what's left.
3. Enter your group details and submit your request.
4. Track your bookings any time from the [Bookings page](/bookings).

Access officers can manage permits, availability and incoming bookings from the admin area.

### Want to offer permits for your cave?
If you look after a cave that needs permits and would like to take bookings through Subterra, get in touch and we'll help you set it up.
