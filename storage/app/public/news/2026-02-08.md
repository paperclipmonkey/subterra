# Feb 8th Update: Routes, Weather & Collections

We've been hard at work adding new features to help you plan your trips better. Here's what's new since January:

### 1. Routes 🗺️
You can now view detailed **Routes** within cave systems. This allows for better planning and navigation underground. Check out the new Routes tab on cave pages!

### 2. Weather & Rainfall 🌧️
We've integrated **River Levels** and **Rainfall Data** directly into the platform. You can now see recent rainfall and river level figures for relevant catchments, helping you make informed decisions about water levels before you head out.

### 3. Collections 📚
Introducing **Collections**! You can now view custom lists of caves. Whether it's the "Best Mines", "Streamways", or "Pretties", you can track your progress through experiencing them all.

---
Stay safe and happy caving! 🦇
