# About Subterra

Subterra is an open-source platform built for cavers, by cavers. Whether you're planning your next descent, logging a trip report, or surveying a new passage, Subterra brings the tools and the community together in one place.

## Our Mission

We want to make caving easier to organise, safer to coordinate, and more rewarding to share. That means:

- **Trip planning** — organise meets, coordinate logistics, and keep everyone on the same page before you go underground.
- **Safety coordination** — know who's going where, and when they're due back out.
- **Data sharing** — record trip reports, cave data, and surveys so the whole community can benefit.

Caving knowledge has always been passed between people. Subterra is built to keep that tradition alive in a way that's open, searchable, and lasting.

## Who We Are

Subterra was created by [Mikey Waterworth](https://subterra.world/profile/tSqCHR8), a caver who saw a need for a modern, community-driven platform to encourage sport caving and help more people explore underground.

It's a community project at heart. Every trip report, edit suggestion, and line of code comes from cavers who want to make the sport better for everyone.

## Open Source

The platform is entirely open-source. We believe community tools should be transparent, accessible, and inclusive — and that the data cavers contribute should belong to the community.

- **GitHub Repository:** [github.com/paperclipmonkey/subterra](https://github.com/paperclipmonkey/subterra)
- **License:** Subterra is open-source software, free to use and free to improve.

## Get Involved

We welcome contributions from everyone, whether you're a developer, a cave surveyor, or just a caver with a story to tell.

- **Add Trip Reports:** Most importantly, use the platform and share your caving experiences — every report makes Subterra more useful.
- **Improve Data Quality:** Spotted incorrect data? Submit a correction using the **Suggest Edit** button on most pages.
- **Suggest Features:** Have an idea for something new? [We'd love to hear it!](mailto:mike@3equals.co.uk)
- **Report Bugs:** If you find something wrong, let us know on [GitHub](https://github.com/paperclipmonkey/subterra/issues).
- **Contribute Code:** Check out our [development guide](https://github.com/paperclipmonkey/subterra/blob/main/DEVELOPMENT.md) to get started.

Thank you for being part of the Subterra community — see you underground!
