# About Subterra

Subterra is an open-source platform built for cavers, by cavers. Our mission is to provide the caving community with tools for trip planning, safety coordination, and data sharing.

## Who We Are

Subterra was created by [Mikey Waterworth](https://subterra.world/profile/3), a caver who saw a need for a modern, community-driven platform to encourage sport caving and to encourage people to explore more.

## Open Source

The platform is entirely open-source. We believe that community tools should be transparent, accessible, and inclusive.

- **GitHub Repository:** [Github.com / Subterra](https://github.com/paperclipmonkey/subterra)
- **License:** Subterra is open-source software.

## Get Involved

We welcome contributions from everyone, whether you're a developer, a cave surveyor, or just a caver with a story to tell.

- **Report Bugs:** If you find something wrong, please let us know on GitHub.
- **Suggest Features:** Have an idea for a new feature? [We'd love to hear it!](mailto:mike@3equals.co.uk)
- **Data Quality:** If you notice incorrect data, you can submit suggestions for most pages using the "Suggest Edit" button on the page.
- **Contribute Code:** Check out our [development guide](https://github.com/paperclipmonkey/subterra/blob/main/DEVELOPMENT.md) on GitHub to get started.
- **Add Trip Reports:** Most importantly, use the platform and share your caving experiences!

Thank you for being part of the Subterra community!
